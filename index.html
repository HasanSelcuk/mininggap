What happens when Bitcoin runs on fees?

Bitcoin pays miners with new coins, called the block subsidy. Every four years it is cut in half, so fees must make up the difference.
Right after a block is found, few fees are left, and miners may wait. This wait is the mining gap.

Follow a miner and an investor to see what makes the mining gap shorter or longer.
